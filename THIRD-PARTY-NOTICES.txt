PKHeX.Core is used for Generation 3 encounter data, RNG seed recovery,
PID/IV correlation rules, and wild encounter slot/frame verification.
It is distributed as an unmodified NuGet dependency. Only selected PID
values are applied; generated Pokemon records are not copied over the
user's data.
